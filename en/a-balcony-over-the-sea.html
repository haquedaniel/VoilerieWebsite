the spirit of the place

A place to follow the light, the boats and the movement of the water.

From the balcony, the view is constantly changing: boats resting at low tide, the harbour coming to life, clouds racing by, then evening light falling over Audierne.

À l’intérieur, l’appartement reste simple, clair et confortable. On y vient pour se poser, cuisiner quelques produits du marché, lire, télétravailler quelques heures ou repartir explorer les plages et les sentiers du Cap Sizun.

This is the apartment most naturally made for contemplation: ideal for a couple or family stay, for a few days breathing in front of the sea.
