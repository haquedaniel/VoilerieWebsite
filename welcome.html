signature experience

A small rowing boat to experience the Goyen differently.

The rowing boat is made available free of charge for guests of Le Clos de la Voilerie, only when weather and tide conditions allow.

To obtain the padlock code, please read the usage rules. They are simple, but important: the estuary is a real marine environment.

  • Use only in calm weather and during daylight.
  • Life jackets are compulsory for all occupants.
  • Children must always be accompanied by an adult.
  • Do not use the rowing boat after consuming alcohol.
  • Check the weather, tide and current before every outing.
  • The rowing boat must be returned to its place, tied up and locked after use.
Padlock code: 4545

This code is reserved for guests of Le Clos de la Voilerie who have read and accepted the rowing boat conditions of use.

our recommendations

Walks, activities and favourite addresses

Here is a selection of walks, activities and food stops that we particularly enjoy in the area.

🥾

Pointe du Raz & tea at Monsieur Papier

Have tea with a good cake at Monsieur Papier at the Pointe du Raz. For us, the most beautiful way to discover the site is to hike from the Pointe du Vent (Trouguer windmill car park), with a magnificent panorama over the Pointe du Vent, the Pointe du Raz, the Île de Sein and the Baie des Trépassés.

🐠

The Audierne aquarium

An ideal activity for children and adults alike. The bird show is really lovely.

🥞

Audierne market

On Wednesday or Saturday morning, have a butter-and-sugar crêpe at Fred’s (the orange food truck), then buy crabs and oysters just opposite.

🚲

Pont-Croix on foot or by bike

A lovely outing, especially on Tuesday evening for the producers’ market and concerts. You can set off from Audierne by bike, with rentals available at l’Escapade Nautique.

🛶

Kayaking in the mouth of the Goyen

A beautiful way to discover the landscape from a different angle. If you are lucky, Randy the dolphin might even make an appearance.

🍸

Sunset at La Renverse

Go for a cocktail at La Renverse in Audierne, enjoy an aperitif platter and watch the sunset.

⛴️

A day on the Île de Sein

Take the boat for a day on this small, mystical, car-free island (50-minute crossing), ideal for children and dogs. We recommend the oyster and seaweed tartare at Brigitte’s on the Quai Paimpolais, then climbing up to the lighthouse for a full view of the island.

⚓

Douarnenez & the Port Rhu

Walk around the Port Rhu and visit the Port Museum. For lunch, we like the fish and chips at Pesket on the quays, or Bigorneau Amoureux for delicious bistro cooking.

Good idea: do not hesitate to stop by the tourist office, located on the market square, for more information about visits.

règlement intérieur

A few simple rules for everyone’s comfort

You are staying in a historic building on the Audierne-Plouhinec harbour, built in 1868. This building once housed the port’s sail-making workshop. Today, the residence is shared by year-round residents, holidaymakers and people travelling for work. For everyone’s comfort, thank you for respecting these few rules.

  • Please respect the calm of the residence, especially in the evening and early morning, between 10:00 pm and 8:00 am.
  • Please take off your shoes inside the apartment.
  • The accommodation is non-smoking.
  • Please avoid noise in the shared areas.
  • Please close the windows, turn off the heating and switch off the lights when you are away.
  • If anything is broken or if there is a problem, please let us know quickly so that we can help.

Departure procedure

  • Departure time: 10:00 am.
  • Strip the beds and gather the used linen in the bathroom.
  • Close all windows and turn off the heating.
  • Empty the bins: the containers are on the right as you leave the building.
  • End-of-stay cleaning is included, but the apartment must be left in a reasonable condition.
  • Put the keys back in the key box and scramble the code.
