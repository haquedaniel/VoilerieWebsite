House rules

A few simple rules for everyone’s comfort

You are staying in a historic building on the Audierne‑Plouhinec harbour. Built in 1868, this building once housed the port’s sail‑making workshop. Today, the residence is shared by year‑round residents, holidaymakers and people travelling for work. For everyone’s comfort, thank you for respecting these few rules.

  • Please respect the calm of the residence, especially in the evening and early morning, between 10:00 pm and 8:00 am.
  • Please take off your shoes inside the apartment.
  • The accommodation is non‑smoking.
  • Please avoid noise in the shared areas.
  • Please close the windows, turn off the heating and switch off the lights when you are away.
  • If anything is broken or if there is a problem, please let us know quickly so that we can help.

Departure procedure

  • Departure time: 10:00 am.
  • Strip the beds and gather the used linen in the bathroom.
  • Close all windows and turn off the heating.
  • Empty the bins: the containers are on the right as you leave the building.
  • End‑of‑stay cleaning is included, but the apartment must be left in a reasonable condition.
  • Put the keys back in the key box and scramble the code.
